Why We Exist

We want to see Christian baseball players unite around the truth of the gospel and walk worthy of the calling with which they have been called. Our mission is to help players realize their identity is rooted in being sons of God, not in their success on the baseball field.

We want to find the guys who are pursuing Jesus and create a space for them to grow and be discipled. We want to encourage them, stir their faith, and see their heart for their teams grow through the revelation that their team is a mission field, and that God's heart is to see all come to know Him.

We want to see the Kingdom of God reach every team, locker room, and organization through learning love God and to love others with all gentleness and lowliness, bearing with one another in love. Our mission is to make disciples who make disciples.

"As a prisoner for the Lord, then, I urge you to live a life worthy of the calling you have received." Ephesians 4:1

What We Do

Community

We host monthly Zoom calls a way to create a community of Jesus followers from all over the country.

Discipleship

We teach guys how to run a discovery Bible study through testimony, gospel talks, and lessons on what following Jesus looks like.

Mission

We allow these guys to learn from faithful leaders so they can be equipped to carry the gospel to their teammates.
